From Sketch To Street: The RUKUS Design Process

How does a garment get built? In our studio, it doesn't start with digital software. It starts with a pencil, a heavy piece of paper, and an idea of what structure we want to capture. We sketch silhouettes that prioritize lines, boxiness, and shoulder structure.

"IF THE DRAFT PATTERN ISN'T BOXING OUT CORRECTLY ON THE CHEST, WE TEAR IT UP AND START AGAIN. STREETWEAR IS ABOUT PROPORTIONS."

Once a sketch is refined, we construct the first prototype in raw canvas. This lets us see how the heavy weight cotton will fall, how the hood stands up (a flat hood is a sin in our studio), and where the dropped shoulder seam should cleanly lay.

FABRIC SELECTION & DYEING

Our fabrics are custom dyed to our specifications to ensure our red matches the exact RGB of our brand system, and our blacks hold deep color without fading after a wash. We wash the cotton pre-production so that the shrink rate is virtually zero for the customer.

FINAL QUALITY ASSURANCE

Every single garment is inspected, numbered, and packaged inside Lagos. We check every double-stitch line and test the zippers before placing the limited label on the piece. We build products that last a lifetime.
