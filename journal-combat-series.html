Behind the Combat Series: Building Our Heaviest Drop Yet

The philosophy behind RUKUS has always been one of weight and presence. When you step out in our clothing, we want you to feel the physical presence of the garment. For the Combat Series, we pushed that boundary further than ever before. We didn't just want a new drop; we wanted a testament to heavy construction.

"WE SOURCED LUXURY 420GSM FRENCH TERRY COTTON AND CUSTOM HARDWARE THAT WE DISTRESSED BY HAND. THIS IS STREETWEAR DESIGNED TO SURVIVE."

Standard hoodies on the market range from 280 to 320GSM. While comfortable, they lack the structural armor-like drape that represents true luxury streetwear. For the Combat Series, we designed a custom weave 420GSM French Terry. It is heavy, structured, and commands respect.

LAGOS INSPIRATION & RAW UTILITY

Lagos is a city of raw energy, speed, and continuous motion. The Combat Series draws heavily from the utility and resilience required to navigate these streets. We integrated functional elements: oversized reinforced cargo pockets, double-layered shoulders for longevity, and hand-distressed hems.

LIMITED RUN, NO RESTOCKS

Following our core ethos, the Combat Series has been produced in extremely limited quantities. We believe in the exclusivity of physical objects in a digital world. Once a piece is gone, it is gone forever.
